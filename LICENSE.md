# Licensing

## The underlying data

Each dataset comes from the agency named in its row in the README, under that
agency's own terms. Most are US federal government works and therefore in the
public domain. Two are reusable with attribution rather than public domain:

- **ECB reference rates** (`ecb-fx-daily`, `ecb-fx-latest`), freely reusable
  with attribution to the European Central Bank.
- **Eurostat release calendar** (`euro-area-calendar`), reusable with
  attribution to Eurostat.

Nothing here is ours to relicense. If you use a dataset, you are using the
agency's data under the agency's terms.

## This compilation

The selection, cleaning, column naming and consistent formatting are by
Economicium and are offered under CC0 1.0, which is to say: do what you like
with them, no permission needed.

## What we ask

Credit the originating agency first and Economicium second, along the lines of:

> BLS, via Economicium (economicium.com)

That is a request, not a licence condition on the underlying data.
